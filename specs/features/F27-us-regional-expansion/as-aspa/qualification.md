# American Samoa public source qualification

The public [American Samoa Priority Climate Action Plan](https://www.aspower.com/ASPAWEB/PublicNotice/CPRG%20PCAP_AS_2024.3.25.pdf), prepared by the territory environmental agency and hosted by ASPA, contains a usable, finite ASPA funding-action subset. Its cover says last revised March 21, 2024; the filename's March 25 value is not the edition date. The source pass closed after twelve minutes and thirty-nine seconds of public text/index review. Untouched PDFs and hashes remain under ignored `inputs/originals/F27-as-aspa/`; `sample.json` records provenance without contact metadata.

## Complete declared enumeration

Printed pages 12–15 / PDF 13–16 identify three ASPA priority actions, with three explicitly numbered funding projects under Action 1. Reconcile all six hierarchical units: retain 1.A National Control Center as capital-project context and 1.C new transmission line to/from it as a transmission project; exclude the Action 1 aggregate, 1.B standalone battery storage, Action 2 vehicles and Action 3 charging infrastructure. The two retained units are related planned funding requests, not a second copy of their parent. The separate ASTCA telecom section is outside this declared ASPA subset. This is not the whole utility or territory's project inventory.

The plan explicitly names the transmission role; no continental voltage threshold is imposed. Native action labels identify planning work units, not engineering assets. Funding secured for the related wind/solar plants does not establish funding or construction approval for the control center or line. The control center's mixed electric/water/wastewater duties remain context, not inferred transmission-only scope. Neither retained unit has a supported voltage, service date, build interval, asset owner or geographic center. Table 5's Tafuna/Tutuila location belongs to the parent action: retain that qualified descriptive context without inventing coordinates, endpoints or a route.

## Newer evidence and remaining gap

- ASPA's [FY2025 audit](https://www.aspower.com/ASPAWEB/PublicNotice/Reports/ASPA%202025%20Final%20Audit%20Reports%20(FS%20%26%20SA).pdf), PDF13/printed11, lists completed Tafuna 15kV switchgear and two generators. Its financial period ends September 30, 2025 and auditor report is dated January 27, 2026. It does not supply a complete planned transmission enumeration or establish the current status of the 2024 requests.
- [RFQ ASPA25.078](https://www.aspower.com/aspaweb/Bids/RFQ%20No.%20ASPA25.078%20-%20T%26D%2035kV%20Transmission%20Line%20Hardware.pdf), issued August 6, 2025, is a hardware supply/delivery purchase. It is not evidence of an additional named physical line project, nor a voltage source for Action 1.C.
- [RFP ASPA26.037](https://www.aspower.com/aspaweb/Bids/RFP%20NO.%20ASPA26.037%20-%20Rate%20Study.pdf), issued August 7, 2026, requests a future rate study across multiple services. It is not an available capital plan or a transmission project.
- The NREL baseline PDF could not be acquired (tool/server failure, then host resolution failure); it is not claimed as inspected or preserved. Official site/procurement indexes are source-discovery context, not project inventories.

Missing enabling input is an accessible current ASPA capital/transmission project register or equivalent named-work inventory with edition, lifecycle, scope and native location/ownership evidence. The PCAP's two records are useful historical proposals, but cannot close territory-wide coverage. Emissions and vehicle-cost estimates concern other measures and are not imported as coordination benefits.
