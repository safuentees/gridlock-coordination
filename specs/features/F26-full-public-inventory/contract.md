## Evidence policy

- Legacy/legacy pairs retain current cross-filing behavior and reasons exactly. Legacy records are not retrospectively claimed to have current status or evidenced distinct owners.
- New records participate only when `recordKind=transmission-project`, lifecycle is planned/approved/under-construction as of the stated edition, a usable center has a qualified role, and owner ID is supported by exact evidence. On-hold, unknown, terminal and capital-context records remain browsable with reasons. Any pair involving a new inventory record requires two evidenced, different owner IDs; legacy sponsor strings or publisher differences are not substitutes. No owner is provided by SCE's Project Manager column, so initial SCE records correctly add inventory rather than invented candidates.
- Native geometry preserves published longitude/latitude pairs as Point/MultiPoint, with role, raw coordinate text, evidence and nullable CRS/accuracy. Unknown CRS and accuracy remain unknown. Interpretation as decimal WGS84 for map display must appear in `locationAssumptions`; all SCE centers stay unverified. One explicit substation/tower point can populate `location`. Exactly two explicitly identified line endpoints use the existing arithmetic midpoint convention. One known endpoint may use single-endpoint with its limitation. Ambiguous point roles, three-plus points or malformed collections retain geometry/evidence but `location=null`, `centerRole=unknown`; never invent a center or route. General pins and permit areas may be mapped with explicit roles but are ineligible for center-distance matching.
- New inventory `centerRole` is the current displayed location's meaning; `nativeGeometry` never changes when a user supplies local evidence. Current local revision form cannot prove role, so root sets centerRole unknown with an explicit local-evidence assumption, requiring a future qualified role revision before eligibility. Legacy location-revision behavior is unchanged.
- Preserve raw status in `status`; normalized lifecycle never derives from an elapsed date. Preserve the source's `date.label`: SCE BF is “Reported projected or actual in-service date”, not a confirmed actual date or construction end. BD construction start is separate `inventory.constructionStart`; `buildWindow=null` without explicit bounds. Multiple-date/partial-date cases remain unresolved or preserve stated precision; no fabricated day.
- Unknown owner/precision/status/coordinates remain null/unknown. Do not infer all project jurisdictions from publisher. Source manifest geographicScope states portfolio extent; later per-jurisdiction allocation needs evidence. Reuse terms have no dataset-specific license declaration; make no blanket license claim.
